Account & Data Deletion

Daily Tracker

How to request deletion

You can delete your data in two ways:

  • In the app: open Daily Tracker → Settings → "Delete Account", and choose to delete your Kids profile, Adult profile, or both. This immediately removes all entries, reminders, and profile information stored on your device.
  • By email (for full account and cloud backup deletion): send a request to the email address below.
dailytrackerapps@app4me.in

Please send this request from the same email address associated with your Google account used in the app, so we can verify and locate your data.

What gets deleted

When you request deletion by email, we will delete:

  • Any backup data stored in your Google Drive app folder (created via the app's optional backup feature)
  • Any account information associated with your request

Data stored only on your own device (entries, reminders, profile info) is already fully under your control and can be removed instantly at any time via the in-app "Delete Account" option — no email request is needed for this.

How long it takes

Email deletion requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted.

Questions?

See our Privacy Policy for full details on what data we collect and how it's used, or contact us at the email above with any questions.

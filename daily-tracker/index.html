Daily Tracker

A simple, private way for families to keep track of health & daily care — for both kids and adults, in one app.

What Daily Tracker does

Daily Tracker helps parents and adults keep a clear, organized record of everyday health and care activities — food, medicine, vaccinations, growth, feeding schedules, and more — with reminders so nothing gets missed. It supports separate profiles for a child and for an adult, so a family can track both in the same place.

🍽️ Food & meal tracking
💊 Medicine reminders
💉 Vaccine & insulin logs
📈 Growth tracking (height/weight)
🍼 Feeding schedules
🩸 Diabetics readings
❤️ Blood pressure & cholesterol
📝 Personal notes & reminders

Reminders that actually work

Set a reminder once — daily, weekly, monthly, or a custom one-time date — and Daily Tracker takes care of the rest, notifying you right on schedule until you turn it off.

Your data, backed up safely

Daily Tracker can back up your data to your own private Google Drive storage, so you never lose your history if you switch phones or reinstall the app. This backup is private to the app — it isn't visible in your regular Drive files, and isn't shared with anyone else.
